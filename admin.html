Payment QR Code (Assets Folder)

The checkout QR code is loaded directly from the local assets/qr-code.png file in your project folder.

Current File: assets/qr-code.png

To update the QR code: Simply copy your screenshot image, rename it to qr-code.png, and place/replace it inside the assets/ folder!
📁 Easy Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Take a screenshot of your eSewa, Fonepay, or Banking QR code.
  2. Rename that image file to qr-code.png.
  3. Paste it inside the D:\Shree Website\assets\ folder (replacing the placeholder).
  4. Deploy or refresh and your new QR code will automatically appear on checkout!
